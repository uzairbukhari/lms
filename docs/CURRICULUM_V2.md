## 1. Structure

**Subject → Topic lesson → Sub-lessons (each with a mini quiz) → Main quiz → next topic lesson unlocks.**

- A **topic lesson** is one full topic (for example "Place value to 100"). Topic lessons in a subject are in a fixed
  order; only the current one is open, later ones show a padlock.
- A topic lesson opens with a hook, a driving question, "Today I will..." goals and a 3-question **Remember** warm-up.
- It holds **3 to 6 sub-lessons** of 10 to 15 minutes. Each is one of four kinds and ends with a **mini quiz** (3 to 5
  questions):
  - `learn`: explainer, pictures and narration for one idea;
  - `try`: hands-on (manipulative, real-life activity or experiment);
  - `play`: brain game or challenge;
  - `use`: a real-world problem or a project milestone.
- When every sub-lesson is done, the **main quiz** opens (15 questions, all nine question types), followed by the
  **"I can..." self-check** (a face per success criterion: not yet, nearly, got it).
- **Unlock rule:** 70% or more on the main quiz unlocks the next topic lesson. Below 70%, the weak skills (from
  `skill_tag`) reopen their sub-lessons as **Practise again**, then she retakes. After two retakes below 70% the parent
  gets a note and can unlock from admin.
- **Strength meter:** mini quizzes and the main quiz feed a per-skill meter (not yet, nearly, secure).
- **Test out:** where the Starting point check-in shows a topic is secure, the child may take the main quiz straight away.
- **No dates or weeks** are shown to the child. Semesters stay; each ends with an exam per subject (2 attempts), opening
  when all of that semester's topic lessons are passed, or on a date the parent sets.
- **Graded or practice:** attempts until the topic lesson is passed are graded (best counts); later ones are practice.

### What happens to existing content

- Week 1 check-ins become each subject's **Starting point** (topic 0).
- Week 2 lessons become sub-lessons of the topic lessons marked *(built part)* below; attempts and points are kept.
- The semester map uses one checkpoint per topic lesson instead of per week.
- The weekly calendar button and per-week trails are removed; admin class unlocks stay as the parent's override.

## 2. Standards used

| Code prefix | Framework | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `NC` | England National Curriculum (Zainab Year 2, Fatima Year 4 with Year 5 stretch) | Main anchor, all subjects |
| `PK` | National Curriculum of Pakistan 2022-23 progression grids: `M-GG-D-NN` maths, `S-GG-D-NN` science, `GK-GG-D-NN` general knowledge | Zainab Grade 1, Fatima Grade 4 |
| `CAM` | Cambridge Primary curriculum frameworks 2020: Maths 0096, Science 0097, English 0058 | Zainab Stage 2, Fatima Stage 4 |
| `CSTA` | CSTA K-12 Computer Science Standards, level 1A (ages 5 to 7) and 1B (ages 8 to 11) | Data & AI |
| `AI4K12` | AI4K12 Five Big Ideas | Data & AI (AI strand) |
| `PYP` | IB PYP transdisciplinary themes | General Knowledge shape |

Codes in the tables are ones read from the official documents. Pakistan codes for topics not listed yet are added when
each topic lesson is built (the grids are on ncc.gov.pk). Cambridge Computing 0059 is not mapped yet (no readable copy).
Cambridge "Thinking and Working" (TWM, TWS) and "Science in Context" (SIC) codes are tagged on activities, not topics.

## 6. App changes (build after approval)

1. **Topic lessons and unlocking** (D1 and Worker): topic lessons with ordered sub-lessons; mini quiz and main quiz
   attempts; 70% unlock; Practise again routing by skill; test out; parent override; graded or practice by status;
   remove week-based visibility.
2. **Child screens:** subject path with padlocks, topic lesson page with sub-lesson cards and strength meter, warm-up,
   "I can" self-check, semester map checkpoints per topic lesson.
3. **Parent screens:** progress by subject, topic and standard (NC, PK, CAM), weak skills, project submissions (photo and
   voice to R2), rubric scoring, Workers AI extras (section 7).
4. **Content:** check-ins to Starting points, Week 2 lessons split into sub-lessons, then a pilot topic lesson (Zainab
   Maths 2 "Place value to 100") before the one-time rebuild of Semester 1.
