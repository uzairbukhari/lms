# Science syllabus: 12 lessons per course (Class 1 = Year 2, Class 4 = Year 4)

Source: Oak National Academy (OGL v3.0). Oak lesson and unit slugs were returned by the Oak tools (misconception graph by unit, programme unit lists, key-stage unit lists). Programmes verified: `science-primary-year-2`, `science-primary-year-4`. Units from Year 1, 3, 5 are marked (Y1/Y3/Y5); their programme slug follows the same pattern (`science-primary-year-N`) but was not fetched separately.
Keywords are the writer's selection from Oak lesson titles and vocabulary (not pulled from the keyword graph).

Conventions in every NEW entry
- Sub-lessons run learn, try, play, use; 2 stops each; quiz themes total 30 (8/8/7/7), mix recall/apply/reason.
- Reason ideas: a named child (Sam, Aisha, Layla, Omar, Zainab, Hamza...) makes a claim, the pupil decides and explains.
- Clip = 45 to 75 second narrated animated story. Unsplash = one concept per query (3+). No Coteach for science.
- Local context follows LESSON_GUIDELINES (UK and Pakistan; Islamic examples only where natural). Facts must be well established and safe (no tasting unknown plants, never look at the Sun).
