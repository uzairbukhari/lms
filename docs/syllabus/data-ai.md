# Syllabus: Data & AI (`data-ai`), Class 1 and Class 4

Conventions
- Class 1 = age_band 7-8, UK Year 2 (KS1). Class 4 = age_band 9-10, UK Year 4 (KS2). Sentences about 12 words (C1) / 16 words (C4).
- Existing lessons keep slug and topic number. New lessons are appended as topics up to 12. New slug pattern `data-ai-class-<1|4>-<kebab-title>`.
- Oak programme slug used = Oak sequence slug taken from Oak unit URLs: `computing-primary` (year-specific programme slugs not verified). Support units from RSHE: `rshe-pshe-primary`.
- Oak has no primary AI unit. AI topics carry `no_oak_unit` plus a reason. Their keywords/misconceptions are marked (own).
- Attribution line for credits page: "Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0." No Oak branding.
- Quiz themes: mix of recall / apply / reason across 9 control types. Reason ideas name a child and a claim.
- Weak Oak coverage: AI topics (C1: 4, 10, 11, 12; C4: 3, 7, 10, 12). Oak units for "events and actions" and "selection quiz" have no published lessons, so they are not used.
