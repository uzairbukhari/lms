# Maths syllabus: 12 lessons per course (Class 1 = Year 2, Class 4 = Year 4)

Source: Oak National Academy (OGL v3.0). All Oak lesson and unit slugs below were returned by the Oak tools (misconception graph by unit, unit list by programme, search). Programme slugs: `maths-primary-year-2`, `maths-primary-year-4`. A unit from another year is marked (Y1/Y3/Y5).
Keywords are the lesson writer's selection from Oak lesson titles and vocabulary (not pulled from the keyword graph).

Conventions used in every NEW entry
- Sub-lessons run learn, try, play, use. Each has 2 stops. Quiz themes total 30 (8/8/7/7), mix recall/apply/reason.
- Reason ideas = a named child (Sam, Aisha, Layla, Omar, Zainab, Hamza, Mira...) makes a claim, the pupil decides and explains.
- Clip = 45 to 75 second narrated animated story, one idea.
- Local context follows LESSON_GUIDELINES (pence and Rs, UK and Pakistan, Islamic examples only where natural).
- Coteach = diagram idea for the Coteach tool (2+ per lesson). Unsplash = one concept per query.
