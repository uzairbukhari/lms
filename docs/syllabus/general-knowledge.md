# Syllabus: General Knowledge (`general-knowledge`), Class 1 and Class 4

Conventions
- Class 1 = age_band 7-8, UK Year 2 (KS1). Class 4 = age_band 9-10, UK Year 4 (KS2). Sentences about 12 words (C1) / 16 words (C4).
- Existing lessons keep slug and topic number (4 each). New lessons are appended as topics 5 to 12. New slug pattern `general-knowledge-class-<1|4>-<kebab-title>`.
- GK blends Oak geography, history, RSHE, religious education (and one science unit as a stretch). Oak programme slugs used = Oak sequence slugs from unit URLs: `geography-primary`, `history-primary`, `rshe-pshe-primary`, `religious-education-primary`, `science-primary` (year-specific programme slugs not verified). Oak citizenship has no primary units.
- Attribution line for credits page: "Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0." No Oak branding.
- Quiz themes: mix of recall / apply / reason across 9 control types. Reason ideas name a child and a claim.
- Weak Oak coverage: C1 "Earth and Sky" (no KS1 space unit; Oak's Earth, Sun and Moon is Year 5 science, used as a stretch); C1 "Caring for Our World" partly (RE not geography); C1 "How People Live" (Oak transport-then-and-now not located). Religious content in Oak RE is plural (Christian, Jewish, Humanist): use only Islamic and neutral lessons with the family, and treat the others as comparison.
- Oak content guidance flags: C4 T12 (discrimination; adult supervision recommended); C1 T9 Al-Razi (illness; adult supervision recommended).
