# Syllabus: English (`english`), Class 1 and Class 4

Conventions
- Class 1 = age_band 7-8, UK Year 2 (KS1). Class 4 = age_band 9-10, UK Year 4 (KS2). Sentences about 12 words (C1) / 16 words (C4).
- Existing lessons keep slug and topic number (C1 topics 1-5, C4 topics 1-4). New lessons are appended up to topic 12. New slug pattern `english-class-<1|4>-<kebab-title>`.
- Oak programme slug used = Oak sequence slug from unit URLs: `english-primary` (year-specific programme slugs not verified). Oak English units are text-based (one book or text type per unit), so topics follow Oak texts and text types.
- Oak's KS1 English unit list tool returned Year 1 only; Year 2 units were found by search (marked Y2). KS2 units found were mostly Year 3 to 6; Year 4 units used wherever they exist, a few Year 3 units for spelling/grammar (marked Y3).
- Attribution line for credits page: "Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0." No Oak branding.
- Quiz themes: mix of recall / apply / reason across 9 control types. Reason ideas name a child and a claim.
- Weak Oak coverage: C1 spelling and homophones (Oak GPC units exist but no homophone unit located); C4 grammar (no Year 4 grammar unit found; Y3 grammar unit used). Some Oak lessons carry "adult supervision recommended" (The Proudest Blue, Escape from Pompeii): flagged below.
