Remember

  • A two-digit number is made of tens and ones. In 47 the 4 means 4 tens, which is 40.
  • To partition a number, split it into tens and ones: 47 = 40 + 7.
  • To compare, look at the tens first. The mouth of < and > eats the bigger number.
